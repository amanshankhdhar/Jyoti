Privacy Policy — Jyoti

Last updated: August 2026

Jyoti is built with privacy as a core pillar. This application requires no internet permission whatsoever. It cannot connect to the internet, download ads, or transmit data to any server.

Permissions Explained:

  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively to access the physical LED flash hardware via the Camera2 API. Jyoti never opens the camera lens, captures photos, records video, or accesses the camera preview. No biometric or visual data is processed.
  • Microphone Permission: Used exclusively for the "Clap-to-Light" feature to detect loud audio spikes. Audio is analyzed locally in RAM for volume levels and is immediately discarded. No audio is recorded, saved, or transmitted.
  • Sensors (Accelerometer/Light): Used locally for "Shake-to-Toggle" and "Auto-on in Darkness". Sensor data never leaves your device.
  • Vibration & Notifications: Used purely for tactile feedback and the persistent "Torch ON" status notification.

Data Collection:

Jyoti collects zero personal data. There are no analytics trackers, no crash reporting tools (like Firebase Crashlytics), and no third-party SDKs. Your usage remains entirely on your local device.

v3.0 Addendum — Internet & Update Check

The INTERNET permission is used solely to query the official GitHub repository for newer releases. No personal data is uploaded, no analytics, no ads.
